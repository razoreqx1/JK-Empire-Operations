Task-first Home, Stations, Plans, History and Settings, with specialist features retained under All Tools, including ship building.
Independent production scenarios: choose an owned recipe, additional modules or extra hourly output, then check and save an advisory plan.
Change the station directly in Plans while retaining recipe and quantity intent; previous calculated results require a fresh check.
More visible recipe choices, explicit quantity labels and plain-language input-supply choices.

Production scenarios do not build modules, spend credits or order ships. External supply is an assumption, not proof of available delivery. Saved player scenarios are advisory snapshots, not live construction progress.
