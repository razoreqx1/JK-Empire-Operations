EOC 3.2 GA
Build 245 | Version 345

COMMAND CHECKLISTS AND RESILIENT DOCK ACCESS

Build 245 turns recovery checklists into bounded two-way command and decision paths across every supported issue family. It also hardens the Docked-menu integration reported by a player without adding a watcher, recurring scan, or per-frame repair.

Highlights
- Docked-menu callbacks are isolated. One failing third-party callback can no longer prevent later callbacks, including EOC's access button, from rendering.
- Bounded Dock lifecycle diagnostics identify menu dispatch, EOC callback presence, callback errors, button rendering, and clicks. Only the first eight menu-display samples are logged.
- EOC-owned checklist steps can request one bounded fresh evidence review. Supported facts remain EOC-verified; blocked evidence is reported rather than guessed.
- Player decision steps cycle through Yes/Done, No/Not Done, and unanswered. A No branch keeps the prerequisite unresolved without independently cancelling or mutating managed actions.
- The command/decision model applies to shortages, overages, production, missing inputs, unavailable supply, missing logistics, and project-driven demand.
- EOC-known supplier and compatible-trader facts are not assigned back to the player. Unsupported rules, prices, blacklists, live orders, and manager range remain clear inspection boundaries.
- Clear All resets checklist answers and command requests with cases and managed actions.
- Stations now says Run Fresh Empire Analysis and reports empire completion before the selected-station result.
- Diagnostics distinguishes ware allocation limits from physical cargo-storage capacity and fixes the 90 percent display.
- Transient case-action messages are cleared when another case opens.
- Player issues: https://github.com/razoreqx1/JK-Empire-Operations/issues. Include EOC build/version, X4 version, reproduction steps, relevant mods, and a fresh debug log.
