# EOC 3.2 GA Release Notes

EOC 3.2 delivers a faster, clearer Executive Operations Center with redesigned KPI intelligence, safer case management, stronger evidence consistency, and expanded fleet visibility.

## KPI Center

- Native Player Wealth, Case Trends, and Empire Growth graphs with 5, 10, and 30-minute ranges.
- Bounded five-row detail pages across cash flow, profit, construction, shortages, attention, trade, logistics, storage, workforce, shipyards, earners, and drains.
- Actual ship workload in Shipyard Activity without station-module construction contaminating ship counts.
- Bounded 30-minute sampling history and page-preserving automatic updates.

## Performance

- Five-minute shipyard reconciliation.
- Two-minute construction automation checks.
- Two-minute ship and remediation matching.
- Bounded UI histories and visible result windows.

## Cases and evidence

- Two-step Clear All EOC Cases action.
- Immediate MONITORING / STABLE station-profile reset.
- One fresh analysis after clearing.
- Visible supporting cases or retained evidence required for non-healthy station classifications.
- Improved player-case evidence handoff, verification, and terminal no-match results.

## Fleet and logistics

- Observation-only visibility for compatible available ships that are not yet EOC-registered.
- Zero-default global minimum policies for mining, trade, build-storage trade, defence, and escorts.
- Player authority modes, normal resources, and existing assignment safeguards remain in force.

## Safety and compatibility

EOC does not create free ships, bypass normal resources, or make unauthorized credit movements. EOC supports X4 8.x and 9.x and does not require UI Extensions and HUD.
