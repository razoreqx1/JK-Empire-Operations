# EOC 1.6 GA Release Notes

EOC 1.6 GA completes the management-window navigation and diagnostic workflow so normal and advanced operations are available directly in the Lua interface while preserving the optional pinwheel.

## Highlights

- Adds **Clear Filters** controls to every filtered EOC page.
- Adds a dedicated **Diagnostics** tab beside Fleet and Logistics with guided recovery, supplier diagnostics, stabilization findings, engineering probes, and Medical Supplies proof status.
- Adds return-to-origin navigation for automatic Report Center redirects, restoring the previous page, selection, filters, and view state.
- Places **Cases** and **Reports** at the end of the operational tab sequence immediately before **Global Settings**.
- Expands Lua access to normal and advanced player workflows while preserving the pinwheel as an optional alternative.
- Preserves immediate report delivery, bounded report history, player authority boundaries, and Mission Director-owned business logic.
- Retains X4: Foundations 8.x and 9.x compatibility.

## Getting started

Press **Control + H** to open EOC. Choose **Open EOC Management Window** for the Lua interface or continue using the EOC pinwheel. Use Stations for role and operational controls, Overview for empire intelligence, Fleet and Logistics for operational support, Diagnostics for guided recovery, Cases for active issues, Reports for completed report history, and Global Settings for authorized automation controls.

## Safety and compatibility

- EOC executes supported actions only within authority granted by the player.
- The original pinwheel remains available as an optional interface.
- EOC does not require another mod to run.
- Removing EOC does not make an existing save dependent on the mod.
