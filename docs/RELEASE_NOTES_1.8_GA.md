# EOC 1.8 GA Release Notes

EOC 1.8 GA adds Personal Office access and the read-only Empire KPI Center while preserving Control + H and player-authority safeguards.

## Highlights

- Adds **OPEN EXECUTIVE OPERATIONS CENTER** to Dock Interactions while seated in the Personal Office chair.
- Opens the same Management Window used by Control + H and returns cleanly with Back or Escape.
- Adds a read-only Empire KPI Center with empire health, ranked station attention, trends, and operational measures.
- Requests player feedback on useful, unclear, and missing KPIs.
- Preserves EOC 1.7 fleet, feedback, navigation, authority, and safety improvements.
- Supports X4 8.x and 9.x and requires **UI Extensions and HUD**.

## Menu and SETA behavior

SETA is not used while EOC or another full-screen menu is open. Exit the menu before activating SETA.

## Upgrade

Steam subscribers update automatically. Manual users must replace JK_Station_Manager, install and enable **UI Extensions and HUD**, restart X4, and wait approximately 10 seconds after loading.
