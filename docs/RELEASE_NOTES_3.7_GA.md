# EOC 3.7 GA — Build 321

EOC 3.7 adds Predictive Intelligence and a guided path from evidence to player action.

## Highlights

- Ranked Predictive views separate actionable issues, watch items, and incomplete evidence.
- Baseline-only observations remain evidence collection until enough separated history exists.
- Exact forecasts open the matching station and ware Case rather than sending the player to a generic page.
- Guided Recovery explains what EOC found, what EOC is doing, what the player needs to do, and how the result will be verified.
- Unified Logistics Coverage supplies stock, target, source, compatible-ship, assignment, and delivery evidence.
- Derived profit exposure is context for the originating incident, not a duplicate alert.
- Navigation preserves the Predictive filter, selected station, and return path.
- Ask EOC requests expose checking and waiting states and reject duplicate submissions.
- An unchanged completed verification escalates to an evidence-backed player check rather than an endless wait loop.
- Primary workflow buttons remain steady; flashing and its timed redraw were removed before GA.

## Authority boundaries

Predictive Intelligence is evidence and navigation. It does not perform background Supply scans, buy or grant ships, steal assigned ships, create resources, move cargo or credits, place station modules, or authorize construction.

Build 321 is the identity-only GA promotion of the human-accepted Build 320 runtime.
